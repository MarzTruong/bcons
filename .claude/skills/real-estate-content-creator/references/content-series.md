# Turning a document into a content series

Input is usually a price list, a PTTT announcement, a training deck, a construction update, or a research report.

1. **Extract the usable facts** into a short list with where each came from (file + section/page). Note what is ambiguous or contradictory between documents — those become placeholders or questions, not content.
2. **Map facts to buyer questions.** Each idea answers one real question a buyer asks ("cần bao nhiêu tiền để bắt đầu?", "PTTT nào lợi nhất nếu mình vay?", "giao nhà khi nào, trong lúc chờ mình trả gì?", "căn nhỏ nhất bao nhiêu?").
3. **Propose 6-10 ideas** as a table:

| # | Ý tưởng | Định dạng | Góc hook | Số liệu dùng (nguồn) | Thiếu gì |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

   Mix formats (video talking head, faceless, FB analysis, listing, Threads) and funnel stages (kéo khách mới / nuôi khách đang cân nhắc / mời đến sự kiện). Suggest an order to post them and why.
4. **Stop and let the user pick**, unless they asked for full drafts up front — then write the top 2-3 fully following video-script.md / social-post.md.

Save the idea table to `docs/content/<date>-y-tuong-<slug>.md` with the fact list and its sources.
